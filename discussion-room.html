Open room

Discussion room

This is the online room for Flavor Forum sessions. You do not need an account. Before you enter, you can type your name and check your camera and microphone.

During a session, the organizers open the meeting and everyone can join. You can share your screen from the bar at the bottom of the call.

Join the room

Opens in a new tab. Works best in a recent version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge.

Before you join

  • Please use your real name.
  • Keep your microphone off when you are not speaking.
  • The room link is public, so do not show private material on screen.
