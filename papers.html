Recent papers

Flavor physics papers from the last two months with at least one author at an institute in India. Open a paper to read it and discuss it with others. No login is needed to comment.

How this list is made

Every morning the list is updated from INSPIRE: new hep-ph, hep-ex and hep-lat papers with an author affiliated to an institute in India, filtered by flavor-physics keywords. The selection is automatic, so it can miss a paper or include one that is off topic. If you notice this, please write to us.

Thank you to arXiv for use of its open access interoperability. Papers are shown from arXiv and remain with their authors.
